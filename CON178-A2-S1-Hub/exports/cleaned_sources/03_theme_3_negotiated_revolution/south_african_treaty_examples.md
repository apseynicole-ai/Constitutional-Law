Bilateral Treaties
These are agreements between South Africa and one other specific country.
1. South Africa–China Comprehensive Strategic Partnership (2024/2025 Framework):
This is a cornerstone of South Africa’s economic policy. In late 2025/early 2026, the two
nations signed the Framework Agreement on Joint Development Economic
Partnership. This agreement focuses on increasing bilateral trade (which reached over
$53 billion in 2025), expanding market access for South African agricultural goods, and
collaborating on "New Quality Productive Forces" like green energy and digital
technology.
2. South Africa–United States Extradition Treaty (2001):
A long-standing and active legal agreement that allows for the formal surrender of
persons accused or convicted of crimes between the two nations. This treaty is
frequently cited in high-profile international legal cases and is supported by a related
Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT) to share evidence in criminal investigations.
Multilateral Treaties
These are agreements involving South Africa and three or more other countries.
1. African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA):
South Africa is a leading member of this landmark agreement, which aims to create a
single continental market for goods and services. In January 2024, South Africa officially
began trading under the AfCFTA preferences, sending its first shipment of locally
manufactured goods to other African ports. It is designed to eliminate 90% of tariffs on
intra-African trade over time.
2. SADC Protocol on Trade (and its updates):
As a member of the Southern African Development Community (SADC), South Africa
is bound by this protocol which established a Free Trade Area among 16 Southern
African nations. It governs the duty-free movement of goods within the region and is a
primary tool for South Africa’s regional economic integration.
