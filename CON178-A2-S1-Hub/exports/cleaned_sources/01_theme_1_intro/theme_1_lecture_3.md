GENERAL BACKGROUND

• The  Constitution  of  the  Republic  of  South  Africa,  1996  is  the

Supreme law of the Republic of South Africa

• Provides legal foundation for the existence of the Country,

• Envisages  a  society  that  is  committed  to  democratic  values,

social justice and fundamental human rights

• Sets out the rights and duties of citizens

• Defines the structures of government


GENERAL BACKGROUND

• For centuries, South Africa was governed by the oppressive systems

of Colonialism and Apartheid

• The  majority  of

the  population  was  disenfranchised  and

disempowered

•

In  the  1990’s  South  Africa  went  through  many  radical  changes,

replacing  the  system  of  Apartheid  with  a  system  of  constitutional

democracy.


GENERAL BACKGROUND

• The  current  Constitution  was  promulgated  by  Nelson

Mandela on the 18th December 1996 and came into effect

on 4 February 1997

• The Constitution has been amended 18 times.

• As  the  highest  law  in  the  country,  NO  other  law  may

contradict or be in conflict with the constitution

The
Preamble


General Purpose of a Pre-amble

• Found at the start of a constitution

• States  the  basic  purpose  of  the  constitution,  underlying

principles, and historical context

• Provisions  not  binding,  but  serve  as  a  guide  to  the

interpretation of the constitution

• Sets out the goals and values of the constitution


Purpose of the SA Constitutions
Pre-amble
• City of Tshwane Metropolitan Municipality v Afriforum [2016] ZACC

“A preamble is after all a succinct expressionary statement that sets
out  a  constitution’s  purpose  and  underlying  philosophy.  By  design
and  like  all  others,  our  Preamble  captures  the  essential  principles
by which we the people seek to govern our affairs.  [The preamble] is
such a crucial part of our Constitution that, if only every citizen were
to internalise it and live according to its terms, our aspirations would
most likely be expeditiously realised.” [para 5]


Key Themes of the Pre-amble

1) Recognition of the Past: Acknowledges the injustices of colonialism

and apartheid.

2) Honouring Heroes: Respects those who struggled for justice and

freedom.

3) Unity in Diversity: Declares South Africa belongs to all who live in it,

united in diversity.

4) Healing & Reconciliation: Aims to heal past divisions.


Key Themes of the Pre-amble

5) Founding Values: Establishes a society based on democratic values,

social justice, and human rights, with equality, non-racialism and non-

sexisim.

6) Open & Democratic Society: Lays the foundations for the rule of law

and government by the people.

7) Improved Quality of Life: Seeks to free the potential of each person.

8 Global Standing: Aims to build a united South Africa, taking its rightful

place internationally.


Recognition of the Past

• Constitutional vision is grounded in the present reality faced

by many South African’s

• Acknowledgement of Colonial and Apartheid Past, which

created an unjust  and unequal Society

• Wealth, resources and status were based on Race, Gender

and Class

• The Constitution seeks to CHANGE this sort of Society
