7. Class activity
The Minister of Forestry, Fisheries and Environment (the
executive) has approved plans for the construction of a new
1200 MW coal-fired powerplant. This comes after a meeting
of the Minister with select foreign energy companies. Only a
small environmental impact assessment was carried out.
The Integrated Resource Plan (IRP) and Carbon Act (CA)
developed by parliament (the legislature) makes provision
for coal fired powerplants. This is provided the
environmental impacts are not serious, and jobs are
prioritised for South Africans. Some MPs that drew up the
policy and act have shares in the energy companies that
attended the meeting. The Minister was not aware of this.

7. Class activity
A group of concerned environmentalists have taken the
matter to court (the judiciary). They argue that a new coal-
fired powerplant infringes their environmental right in
section 24 of the Constitution. Further, they argue
government should be prioritising building new cleaner forms
of energy production in the IRP and the CA.
